## 🎥 1. Skrip Video Demo 60 Detik (Loom / Screen Recording)

Rekam layar Anda menggunakan [Loom.com](https://www.loom.com) atau OBS selama 60–90 detik. Tunjukkan wajah Anda di pojok kecil (face bubble) agar membangun trust instan.

### Alur Demonstrasi Layar:
1. **Detik 00–15: Hero & Marquee Ticker**
   - *Tindakan di Layar:* Buka homepage `atelier-noir-fashion.myshopify.com`, tunjukkan marquee announcement bergerak mulus, scroll ke banner hero dengan tipografi besar dan badge "Autumn/Winter 2026 Drop".
   - *Skrip Suara:*
     > *"Hi [Client Name]! I wanted to show you a quick look at a custom Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme I recently engineered for high-end fashion and lifestyle brands. As you can see, the aesthetics are completely on par with luxury brands like Fear of God or Jacquemus, but what’s most impressive is under the hood: this entire experience was built with zero third-party app bloat."*

2. **Detik 15–35: Lookbook Runway Ensemble & Curated Grid**
   - *Tindakan di Layar:* Scroll ke bagian *Curated Wardrobe*, hover pada produk (tunjukkan gambar berubah otomatis), lalu scroll ke *Editorial Spotlight (Runway Ensemble)* dan tunjukkan breakdown baju yang dipakai model beserta tombol add/view.
   - *Skrip Suara:*
     > *"Instead of using glitchy third-party hotspot apps that break on mobile, I coded a native split-screen Editorial Runway Ensemble. Shoppers can see the complete look and immediately purchase each piece, which significantly boosts multi-item cart sizes."*

3. **Detik 35–55: Product Detail Page, Sizing Modal & AJAX Cart Drawer**
   - *Tindakan di Layar:* Klik salah satu produk (misal: *Double-Breasted Wool Trench*). Buka modal **Size Guide**, klik tombol switch **Centimeters (CM)** ke **Inches (IN)**. Tutup modal. Klik **Add to Bag** -> Cart Drawer terbuka otomatis dan tunjukkan **Free Shipping Progress Meter** bertambah!
   - *Skrip Suara:*
     > *"On the product page, we have a custom interactive Size Guide modal with an instant CM-to-Inches unit toggle that's centered perfectly across all devices. When I add this to bag, our AJAX slide-out cart calculates the exact progress toward our $150 free shipping threshold in real-time. This alone drives a 15–20% bump in Average Order Value without paying a monthly app subscription."*

4. **Detik 55–75: Mobile Responsive & Call-To-Action**
   - *Tindakan di Layar:* Buka Chrome DevTools (Inspect Mode -> iPhone 14 Pro), scroll ke bawah di halaman produk dan tunjukkan **Sticky Mobile Add-to-Cart Bar** yang meluncur mulus dari bawah.
   - *Skrip Suara:*
     > *"Over 75% of your shoppers are on mobile, so I built a native sticky bottom Add-to-Cart bar that ensures the buy button is always one tap away. I'd love to help upgrade your store with this level of performance and luxury polish. Let's hop on a quick 10-minute call to discuss your upcoming collection drop!"*
